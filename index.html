About Us

Growing With Purpose,
Harvesting With Care

Ema Farming is a proudly local agricultural enterprise dedicated to producing fresh, high-quality produce and livestock using sustainable farming practices.

Under the leadership of Karabo Shilakoe, our team works tirelessly to bring the very best of the farm to families, retailers, and businesses across the region.

  • 100% natural, chemical-free growing practices
  • Ethically raised livestock & poultry
  • Reliable delivery & bulk supply options
  • Community-focused & environmentally responsible
